How It Works

From Seed to Investment in 4 Steps

A transparent, secure process for both farmers and investors.

01

Create an Account

Register as a farmer or investor. All accounts are verified before activation.

02

Submit a Campaign

Farmers submit crop details, funding targets, location, and supporting documents.

03

Verification & Approval

Administrators review and verify campaigns before they go live on the platform.

04

Invest & Earn

Investors fund campaigns via GCash, Maya, or Bank Transfer and earn profit on harvest.
