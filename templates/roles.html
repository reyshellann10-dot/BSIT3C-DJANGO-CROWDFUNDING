Who Is It For?

Three Roles, One Platform

CrowdFarmV3 serves farmers, investors, and administrators with distinct, role-specific experiences.

Farmer

For Farmers & Creators

Raise funds for your agricultural campaigns directly from investors. Submit your crop plan, get verified, and start collecting investments.

  • Submit and manage campaigns
  • Upload verification documents
  • Track funding progress
  • Receive investment funds
Investor

For Investors

Browse verified campaigns, invest your money, and earn profit returns when farmers harvest. Full transparency on every peso invested.

  • Browse active campaigns
  • Invest via GCash, Maya, or Bank
  • Track investment portfolio
  • Earn competitive profit returns
Admin

For Administrators

Full system oversight — verify campaigns, manage users, track all investments, and maintain platform integrity.

  • Verify and approve campaigns
  • Manage all users
  • View system-wide analytics
  • Access full audit trail
